## 2026

### 1. Spatially Robust Land Cover Classification with Multi-Seasonal Sentinel-2 Imagery: A Comparison of CNN, UNet++, ConvNeXt and ViT

- **Journal:** *Remote Sensing* 18, 2463 (2026)
- **Role:** First author
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.3390/rs18152463
- **PDF:** [Read article](2026_RemoteSensing_LandCover_Spatial_Transfer.pdf)
- **Summary:** Comparison of CNN, UNet++, ConvNeXt and ViT for five-class land-cover mapping using four seasonal Sentinel-2 composites. Evaluation used strict four-fold spatial cross-validation in the Lake Kerkini region and an additional transferability test at Lake Doirani without fine-tuning. CNN achieved fold-mean overall accuracy of 81.53% and Macro-F1 of 80.09% in the primary study area.

### 2. Operational Wheat-Yield Estimation in the Eastern Mediterranean Using Multi-Temporal Sentinel-2 Imagery and Explainable Machine Learning

- **Journal:** *Algorithms* 19, 392 (2026)
- **Role:** First author
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.3390/a19050392
- **PDF:** [Read article](2026_Algorithms_Wheat_Yield_Explainable_ML.pdf)
- **Summary:** Evaluation of six regression models for predicting wheat yield in nine fields near Ptolemaida using Sentinel-2 time series and Leave-One-Field-Out validation. Best reported Random Forest performance: MAPE 7.90% and RMSE 45.15 kg per decare under the Sentinel-only configuration. SHAP was used to interpret spectral and seasonal feature contributions.

### 3. Development of a Spectral Index and Web Application for Automated Marble Quarry Monitoring: A Sentinel-2-Based Approach for Change Detection and Monitoring

- **Journal:** *Journal of Sustainable Mining* 25(3), Article 10 (2026)
- **Role:** Co-author
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.46873/2300-3960.1515
- **PDF:** [Read article](2026_JSM_Marble_Quarry_Change_Detection.pdf)
- **Summary:** Introduces the Quarry Change Detection Index (QCDI) and an interactive workflow using Google Earth Engine and Streamlit for monitoring marble quarry expansion with Sentinel-2 imagery.
- **Rights note:** The journal landing page states CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; retain the published article unchanged, with the citation and license information. Do not assume this license applies to associated code or other materials.

## 2025

### 4. A Comparative Analysis of U-Net Architectures with Dimensionality Reduction for Agricultural Crop Classification Using Hyperspectral Data

- **Journal:** *Algorithms* 18, 588 (2025)
- **Role:** First author
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.3390/a18090588
- **PDF:** [Read article](2025_Algorithms_EnMAP_UNet_Comparison.pdf)
- **Summary:** Agricultural crop semantic segmentation with EnMAP hyperspectral imagery around Lake Vegoritida. Compared U-Net, U-Net++ and Atrous U-Net with spectral dimensionality reduction and feature-selection methods including LDA and SHAP-based selection. The highest mean F1 score reported was 0.77 for the U-Net++/ACS configuration.

### 5. A Python Framework for Crop Yield Estimation Using Sentinel-2 Satellite Data

- **Journal:** *Earth* 6, 15 (2025)
- **Role:** Co-author
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.3390/earth6010015
- **PDF:** [Read article](2025_Earth_Sentinel2_Python_Framework.pdf)
- **Summary:** Python and Google Earth Engine workflow for automated Sentinel-2 crop monitoring, vegetation-index time series and a web application. The study explores vegetation-index curves and wheat-yield estimation, highlighting GNDVI and LAI.
